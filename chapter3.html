CHAPTER 03 · COLLECTING MEMORIES

Little Escapes ✈️

New places, little adventures, unforgettable memories.

Some of my favorite memories begin with a train ticket, a little curiosity, and no idea how beautiful the day is going to be.

Italy gave me moments I will always remember: wandering through beautiful streets, discovering new places, enjoying the atmosphere, and collecting little memories along the way.

Then there are places like Praha and Dresden, each with its own charm, stories, and corners waiting to be explored.

I love walking around a new city, finding unexpected places, and sometimes simply enjoying the freedom of being somewhere I have never been before.

Not every adventure needs a perfect plan. Sometimes, the best memories happen when you just go.

Travel diary note 📍

Collect moments, not just destinations. The little things often become the biggest memories.
