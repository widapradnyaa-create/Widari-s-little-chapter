About Me ♥

Hi, I'm Ni Putu Widari Pradnyani, also known as Widari!

Marketing grad turned trading consultant turned... hotel person? Yepp, my career took a few plot twists.

I studied Marketing Management, spent some time in the trading consultancy world, and now I'm doing my Study with The Ritz-Carlton, Wolfsburg in Germany. Yes, the fancy one

And because one plot twist is never enough, I'm also learning to code! This website is my very first coding project!

Marketing taught me how to tell a good story. Trading taught me to keep calm when things get hectic. Hospitality is teaching me how to make people feel special. Coding is teaching me how to build things from scratch. Mix those together and you get me! ♡

Outside of work, you'll find me at home or getting lost in a new German city.
