CHAPTER 02 · MY SECOND HOME

Finding Home in Germany 🇩🇪

Sometimes, home is not a place. It's people.

Moving to another country means starting a new chapter without knowing exactly what comes next.

When I came to Germany, everything felt different: the language, the culture, the weather, and the everyday life. I was far away from my family and the place I called home.

But along the way, I met people who made this foreign country feel a little less foreign. My host family and the people I met here became an important part of my journey.

They reminded me that even when you live far away from home, you can still find warmth, kindness, and a sense of belonging.

A little reminder 💌

Home isn't always where your story begins. Sometimes, it's where you find people who make you feel that you belong.
