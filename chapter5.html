CHAPTER 05 · OUR ROOTS, OUR PRIDE

STT Bali–Jerman 🌺

Keeping our culture alive, even miles away from home.

Living far away from Bali does not mean leaving our traditions and culture behind.

STT Bali–Jerman is a reminder that even in a different country, we can stay connected to our roots, celebrate our traditions, and create a sense of togetherness.

Through shared experiences, cultural activities, and moments spent together, we create memories that connect us to Bali and to one another.

Being part of a community also means finding people who understand where you come from and sharing that part of yourself with others.

No matter how far away we are, our culture continues through the people who cherish it and keep it alive.

Our little reminder 🌺

Wherever life takes us, our roots remain part of who we are. Together, we keep our traditions alive.
