CHAPTER 01 · MY ROOTS

From Bali, With Love 🌴

A little piece of home, wherever I go.

Every story starts somewhere. Mine starts on a beautiful island called Bali.

Bali is more than just a place on the map. It's where my story began, where my roots are, and where so many of my favorite memories live.

No matter how far I travel or where life takes me, a little piece of Bali will always travel with me.

Little reminder 💌

You can travel around the world, but home will always have a special place in your heart.
