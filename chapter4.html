CHAPTER 04 · GROWING THROUGH LIFE

Anak Rantau di Jerman 💌

Far from home, closer to the person I want to become.

Moving to Germany was more than moving to another country. It was the beginning of learning how to build a life far away from everything familiar.

Living abroad means learning to do things on your own, adapting to a different culture, finding your way through a new language, and figuring things out one day at a time.

Some days feel exciting. Other days, I miss home, my family, familiar food, and the comfort of knowing that the people I love are just around the corner.

But every challenge teaches me something. Every new experience helps me grow. And every small achievement reminds me why I started.

I am still learning, still making mistakes, and still discovering who I want to become. Maybe that is what growing up really looks like.

A note to myself 🩷

Be proud of how far you have come. You are building a life you once could only imagine. Take your time. You are allowed to grow at your own pace.
